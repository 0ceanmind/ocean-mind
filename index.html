What makes us different

Three things no other course platform does.

OceanMind is not another video library. It is a single interactive system built around one idea: learning AI has depth, and most people never get below the surface.

01

The Iceberg Navigation System

A living, interactive iceberg is the homepage. Each layer is a depth of learning. Learners explore a layer to see what lives at that depth. It is the brand, the product, and the marketing hook in one gesture.

02

The tool is the lesson

Every lesson ships with a live interactive: a neural net you train, a prompt studio wired to a real model, a token visualizer, an attention diagram you poke. The AI is embedded inside the lesson, not described beside it.

03

Addictive by design

XP, streaks, achievements, and visual depth progression turn studying into descent. The goal is simple and ambitious: make learning AI the most compelling thing a person does online.

The product

Six depths. One descent.

Hover any depth to surface what learning lives there. The deeper you go, the more advanced it gets. This is the core navigation of the entire platform.

Competitive landscape

Everyone teaches AI. No one teaches it like this.

The category is crowded with video libraries and code drills. OceanMind competes on a different axis: interactivity as the lesson, depth as the map, and AI embedded throughout.

Business model

Two engines, one platform.

Consumer subscriptions build the brand and the funnel. Organization licensing builds the revenue floor. A third engine opens in Year 2.

Engine 01

B2C subscriptions

A free tier for discovery, then Explorer and Deep Diver plans that unlock every AI tool, certificates, and the full depth of the iceberg. Monthly or annual.

Engine 02

B2B licensing

Per-seat plans for teams, plus Enterprise and Education tiers with SSO, SCORM, white-label, and dedicated support. Org seats carry Explorer-equivalent access.

Engine 03 · Year 2+

Certification & white-label

AI certification programs, and white-label licensing of the interactive lesson engine to publishers and enterprises who want our depth model under their own brand.
